Sandbox where I test and prototype different mechanics.

Gameplay can be seen here: https://youtu.be/06HgEmSv-TU

Combat: Attack, dodge, parry, block and throw weapons.

Enemy AI: Enemies investigate and actively search for you, communicating with each other.

Stealth: Hide in shadows or bushes, throw distractions, and deal critical damage to unsuspecting enemies.

Social stealth: Become invisible by blending in crowds and using disguises.

Interactive environment: Turn off or destroy light sources and vegetation.

Elemental damage: weapons and tools can apply different effects to targets,like fire, poison electricity or bleeding.

Improvise: Water makes electricity deadly, so watch out for puddles.


Controls (mouse and keyboard / pad):

Movement:...................WSAD/Left Thumbstick

Aim:........................Mouse/Right Thumbstick

Light Attack:...............LMB/R1

Strong Attack:..............LMB/R1 (hold)

Throw:......................MMB/R2

Parry/Guard:................RMB/L1

Jump:.......................Space/Button south

Pickup weapon:..............E/Button north

Interact:...................F/Button west

Change disguise:............F/Button west (next to a body)

Dodge:......................Shift/Button east

Noctovision:................N/R3
